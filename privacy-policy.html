Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 24, 2026

This policy explains how independent developer onigoro ("the operator") handles data in connection with the platform API integrations (such as TikTok) used to produce and distribute the video content series "Japan in 60" ("the Service").

1. Nature of the Service

The Service produces short video content featuring landmarks across Japan and distributes it on platforms such as YouTube and TikTok. Videos are posted through platform APIs from official accounts managed by the operator.

2. Information collected

The Service obtains access tokens and account identifiers required to use platform APIs such as TikTok's, through each platform's own authorization flow (e.g. OAuth). It does not currently collect personal information from any other third party, including viewers or general users.

3. Purpose of use

Information obtained is used solely to post video content to the official accounts managed by the operator. It is never provided or sold to third parties.

4. Storage and management

Access tokens and other credentials are stored in environments controlled by the operator (local environments or cloud environments under the operator's own contract) and managed so third parties cannot access them.

5. Changes to this policy

This policy may be changed without prior notice as the Service's features or specifications change. Changes take effect once posted on this page.
